A festival for people who build things that move.

Robofest is the annual robotics festival run by ROBOTiX Club. Students bring circuits, code and a lot of duct tape, and leave with trophies, new teammates and a few good stories.

Whether this is your first robot or your tenth, there is a bracket for you, plus workshops where you can learn sensors, motors and Arduino from scratch.
